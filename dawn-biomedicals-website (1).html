Company overview

A regional supplier, built for a growing healthcare hub

Dawn Biomedicals is a trusted and growing healthcare solutions provider based in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra. The company specialises in the supply and distribution of hospital equipment, healthcare products and scientific laboratory instruments.

As the city's industrial and healthcare infrastructure expands, Dawn Biomedicals supports hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres, research institutions and medical colleges with advanced, cost-effective medical solutions — backed by a commitment to quality, reliability and customer satisfaction.

Vision

To become a leading and reliable supplier of healthcare products across India by delivering innovative, high-quality and affordable products.

Mission

To provide superior medical and laboratory equipment and educational scientific products that meet global standards, with timely delivery and excellent after-sales support — building long-term relationships that improve healthcare infrastructure and patient care.
